Air-Powered Vehicle

MATLABANSYSESP32PneumaticsTIG welding

Remotely controlled vehicle propelled solely by 10-bar compressed air, achieving 4th place out of 64 teams.

  • Performed FEA via ANSYS to validate a lightweight chassis and 3-liter tank with safety factor of 2.
  • Developed an ESP32-based PCB to manage propulsion valves and braking servos with ~30 cm stop distance.
